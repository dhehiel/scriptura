# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Scriptura a respectful and productive experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## A note specific to this project

Scriptura works with material that communities hold sacred, and with questions on which sincere, learned people
have disagreed for centuries. Contributors will hold incompatible views on canon, translation, textual
criticism, historicity, and doctrine.

**That disagreement is the subject matter, not a conduct problem.**

The project's response to disagreement is to record it: competing readings, competing analyses, and competing
interpretations are stored with their attributions and their sources, and presented together. Scriptura does not
adjudicate doctrine and does not take confessional sides.

What is not acceptable is treating a person badly because of the position they hold. Argue about the evidence.
Do not argue about the person.

Contributors should expect their textual, historical, linguistic, and theological positions to be recorded as
attributed positions rather than adopted as project positions. That is the design, not a slight.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and for research integrity
- Citing sources and giving credit where it is due
- Correcting your own errors promptly and visibly
- Distinguishing clearly between what a source says and what you believe

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, including about religious belief or non-belief
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments about a tradition, denomination, or community
- Proselytising, or pressuring others to adopt a confessional position
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Presenting a personal doctrinal position as though it were a project finding
- Fabricating or misattributing sources
- Presenting machine-generated inference as authoritative
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers through
the contact channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will
result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project team is obligated to
maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

### Enforcement ladder

1. **Correction** — private, clear feedback about why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be
   requested.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved
   for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — temporary removal from participation, from days to weeks.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent removal from participation.

Research-integrity violations — fabricated citations, misattributed sources, licence misrepresentation,
model-generated text presented as scripture — are handled as both conduct and integrity matters and may proceed
directly to warning or beyond depending on severity and intent.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is representing the
project or its community in public spaces. Examples include using an official project e-mail address, posting
via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
